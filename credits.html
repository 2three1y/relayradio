Starred but not used

bitcoin-connect

  • Repo: getAlby/bitcoin-connect
  • License: MIT
  • Why not: Starred and allowed, but connecting a wallet adds a lot of weight for one key. Z opens the note in a Nostr app (via njump.me) where you can zap with your own wallet.

Also

  • Browser features: Web Speech, Web Audio, Media Session and the Vibration API.
  • "Open note" links go to njump.me, a public Nostr viewer. Nothing from it is copied here.
  • Relay Radio itself is MIT licensed. See LICENSE and NOTICE in the repo.
