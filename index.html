      About

      Relay Radio listens to public Nostr relays and turns what it hears into sound. It never posts, likes or zaps anything for you, and it never shows images or video. By default it listens to a few curated relays that only carry posts from members or a web of trust. Notes marked sensitive (NIP-36 content warnings), notes tagged NSFW, notes with sexual words or slurs, and accounts that call themselves adult are always skipped, and you can mute more words in Settings. The filter is best effort: Nostr is an open network, so if something slips through, press N to skip it.
